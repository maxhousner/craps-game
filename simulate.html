Results

What these numbers mean
Average result
Profit or loss per session on average, which is your expected result. The ± is the margin of error (95% confidence); more sessions shrink it.
Median session
Half the sessions did better than this and half did worse. When it's far from the average, a few big sessions are pulling the average.
Middle 50% / 90%
The range that the middle half (or 90%) of sessions ended in. This is the swing to expect on a normal night.
Total bet (action)
Everything you had riding on bets that won or lost during a session. More action means more exposure to the house edge.
House edge you paid
Total lost ÷ total action. Compare it with the house-edge table in the guide.
Worst drop
The biggest fall from a session's high point to a later low. Your bankroll has to survive swings this size.
Low point
How far down the session went at its worst moment, on average.
Ran out of money
Sessions that ended because the bankroll couldn't cover the strategy's next bet.
